About Us

Sevier County's Cabin Repair Specialists

Hickory Home and Cabin Repair was built by locals who understand the Smoky Mountain rental market — and what it takes to keep a cabin guest-ready every single day.

Our Story

Built for Cabin Owners, By People Who Get It

Sevier County has one of the most vibrant short-term rental markets in the country. With thousands of cabins on platforms like Airbnb and VRBO, keeping properties in top shape is a constant challenge for owners.

We started Hickory Home and Cabin Repair because we saw cabin owners struggling to find reliable, responsive repair companies. Too many contractors didn't understand the urgency of a rental property — that a broken hot tub on a Friday afternoon means a real financial loss.

So we built a company specifically designed to serve this market: fast response, transparent pricing, and a team that handles everything from hot tubs to HVAC so you don't have to coordinate multiple contractors.

  • Locally owned and operated in Sevier County
  • Over 10 years of combined trade experience
  • Serving 500+ cabin properties
  • Specialized in short-term rental maintenance

Our Core Values

Cabin Owners First

We built this business specifically for short-term rental operators. We understand that a broken appliance doesn't just cause inconvenience — it costs you bookings and reviews.

Honest, Transparent Service

We tell you what's wrong, what it will cost to fix, and what to expect — before we start any work. No hidden fees. No pressure upsells.

Fast Response

When you call, we pick up. We understand urgency and we do everything we can to get someone out to your property the same day or next morning.

Locally Owned
