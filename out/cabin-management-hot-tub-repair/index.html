A Local Maintenance Partner

We're not just a hot tub repair company. We handle hot tubs, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and general cabin maintenance — which means we can be a single point of contact for multiple types of repairs across your portfolio of properties.

When a guest reports a problem, you don't always know right away whether it's a hot tub issue, an electrical problem, or something else. Because we work across all these trades, we can diagnose the actual cause and fix it without you having to coordinate multiple vendors.

Our goal is to help you reduce guest complaints, protect your rental reviews, and keep your properties running smoothly between guest stays.

Common Questions

FAQs for Property Managers

Do you work with cabin management companies?

Yes. We work with cabin management companies and property managers throughout the Smoky Mountain area. We provide priority service, repair documentation, and communication designed for managing multiple rental properties.

Can you service multiple properties under one account?

Yes. We can service multiple rental properties for a single management company. We track each property and its equipment so we have context when a problem is reported.

Do you provide repair documentation?

Yes. We provide repair documentation and photos from service calls so property managers have records for owners and for their own internal tracking.

Can you coordinate with our existing maintenance team?

Yes. We can work alongside your existing maintenance team, handling the hot tub, HVAC, electrical, and plumbing repairs that go beyond routine guest-turnover tasks.

Need a Hot Tub Repair Partner?

If you manage vacation rental cabins in the Smoky Mountains, we can be your local repair and maintenance resource. Call us to discuss a vendor relationship.
