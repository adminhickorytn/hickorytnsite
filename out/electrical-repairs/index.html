Signs You Need Service

  • Outlets that are discolored, warm, or sparking
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly
  • Flickering lights throughout the cabin
  • No power to specific rooms or outlets
  • Burning smell near outlets or panels
  • Outdated two-prong outlets without grounding
  • Hot tub or appliance not receiving power
  • Panel is a Federal Pacific or Zinsco (known fire risk)
  • Hot tub or HVAC on a shared circuit

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is electrical work safe for me to DIY in a rental cabin?

We strongly advise against DIY electrical work in rental properties. Improper wiring is a fire hazard and can violate local codes, putting your guests and your property at risk. Our work is performed under Tennessee CE Electrical Contractor License #86291 and meets current NEC code.

Can you upgrade my cabin electrical panel?

Yes, we handle panel upgrades and replacements for cabins that have grown in electrical demand — especially with hot tubs, EV chargers, or added HVAC systems. We pull permits and pass inspection on the first try.

What is a GFCI outlet and why does my cabin need them?

GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) outlets protect against electrical shock in wet areas. They are required by code in bathrooms, kitchens, and outdoor areas — and essential for guest safety.

How quickly can you respond to an electrical emergency?

We offer same-day service for electrical emergencies across Sevier County. Call 865-805-2438 immediately if you have a sparking outlet, burning smell, or repeatedly tripping breakers.

Are you a licensed electrical contractor?

Yes. Hickory Home and Cabin Repair holds Tennessee CE Electrical Contractor License #86291. All electrical work is performed within the scope of that license and applicable local code requirements.
